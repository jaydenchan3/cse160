Notes to Grader:

  • Blocky peacock with body, neck, head, beak, two legs (upper/lower/foot), tail base, and 5 tail feathers.
  • Sliders for global rotation, both legs (hip / knee / ankle), neck, head, and tail sway.
  • Animation ON by default. Legs walk out of phase with knee bend on the forward swing, neck and head bob, tail sways.
  • Animation ON / OFF buttons work. Sliders drive the peacock when animation is off.
  • Click and drag on the canvas to rotate (horizontal = Y axis, vertical = X axis).
  • Shift-click the canvas for a ~2 second poke: tail fans wide open and the peacock shakes.
  • Neck is a cylinder (non-cube primitive), generated once at init.
  • FPS shown at the bottom of the page.
